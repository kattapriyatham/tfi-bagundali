# Privacy Policy for TFI Bagundaali

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12

TFI Bagundaali ("the app") is a Telugu cinema party card game. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

## Data we collect

**Anonymous account ID.** On first launch, the app signs you in anonymously via Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier tied to your device — no name, email, phone number, or other personal information is collected or required.

**Gameplay stats.** To track your games played, games won, and online matches played, the app stores these counters in Firebase Firestore, linked to your anonymous ID.

**Online multiplayer session data.** When you create or join an online room, temporary game-session data (room code, player list, round state) is stored in Firebase Realtime Database, linked to your anonymous ID. This data is tied to the game session and is not used for any other purpose.

**Local device storage.** Settings (haptics, sound) and your best solo time are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

**Advertising data.** The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, general location (derived from IP), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses data in ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for details.

## What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, or precise location.

## How data is used

- Gameplay stats: to show you your own progress.
- Online session data: to run multiplayer matches.
- Advertising data: to serve and measure ads that keep the app free.

We do not sell your data.

## Third-party services

- **Firebase** (Google) — authentication, database. [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** — advertising. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data deletion

You can permanently delete your stats and anonymous account at any time from **Settings → Delete my data** in the app. This removes your Firestore stats document, your locally stored best time, and your Firebase Auth account. This action cannot be undone.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
