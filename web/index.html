Frequently asked.

Do I need to know Telugu cinema?

It helps, but it is not a trivia quiz. You are matching pictures, not naming films. Knowing your heroes and moments just makes your eyes half a second faster — which is the whole game.

How many people can play?

Solo against the clock, two players on one phone, or an online room with up to eight friends.

Does it cost anything?

Free to play. Ads keep it that way.

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-up, no email, no password. Online rooms use an anonymous ID created on your device so your stats can follow you.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings → Delete my data permanently removes your stats and your anonymous account. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Which platforms?

Android first, arriving on Google Play. More to follow.
